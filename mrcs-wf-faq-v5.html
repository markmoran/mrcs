How do I request my medical records? −

To request your medical records, contact our records department at the main office during business hours. We accept requests in person, by phone, or via our online request form. There is no charge for standard copies, and we typically process requests within 5 to 10 business days. All requests must include your full name, date of birth, and the specific records you need.

For expedited requests or records dating back more than seven years, there may be additional processing time. If you have any questions about your records or need assistance with the request process, our staff is happy to help guide you through each step.
